# Deviations from SAP v4 (appended, never rewritten)

## D-34 (2026-11-17, recorded before the rerun)
During the run, the output of the 26th mutant contained extremely large coordinates. The exact arithmetic of the definition checks raised OverflowError while *evaluating* that output, and the uncaught exception stopped the runner. Fix (in `study4/code/run_study4.py`): if any evaluation step of a completed run raises (comparison with the reference, definition checks, difference from Shapely, metamorphic distance), the step is recorded as not evaluable and the output counts as failing the reference, flagged by the definition checks, differing from Shapely by more than τ and detected by that relation. This favours the oracle-free tests and works against predictions P1 and P3. The 26 completed records are kept; the run resumes with the remaining mutants. Decision rules (Section 5) are unchanged. The numbers of non-evaluable runs and mutants will be reported.

## D-35 (2026-11-17, recorded before the rerun)
After D-34, the exact evaluation of outputs with extreme coordinates did not finish within 20 minutes for some mutants (the 5 s limit of SAP v4 applies to running the subject, not to evaluating its output). Fix: each evaluation step of a completed run gets a 60 s limit; a step that exceeds it is not evaluable and counted as in D-34 (favouring the oracle-free tests). Mutants whose evaluation was interrupted are rerun from the start. Decision rules are unchanged.
